**Block 1.** At forty-one thousand feet, both engines of this brand new jet went silent. The fuel tanks were completely empty.
**Block 2.** The fuel gauge was broken, so the crew did the math by hand. But this jet measured fuel in kilograms, not pounds.
**Block 3.** Mixing them up, the crew loaded less than half the fuel they needed. Halfway to Edmonton, the jet ran dry.
**Block 4.** Luckily, Captain Bob Pearson was also a glider pilot. He flew the silent jet for seventeen minutes, looking for a runway.
**Block 5.** His co-pilot knew an old air base at Gimli, now a racetrack. All sixty-nine survived, and the jet got a nickname: the Gimli Glider.
