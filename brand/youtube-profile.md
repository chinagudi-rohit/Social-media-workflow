# YouTube channel setup: Worth Knowing

One brand that covers every niche the autopilot posts: money (investing, credit, insurance,
real estate, side income, legal money), AI tools, how things are made, history, space and
science, psychology. "Worth" leans to money, "Knowing" to everything else.

Images are in `brand/out/`, rendered from `brand/brand.json` by
`node brand/render.mjs` (change the name there and re-run if the handle is taken).

### Description (paste as is, 660 of 1,000 characters)

```
Worth Knowing explains the things nobody taught you, in about 80 seconds.

What you'll learn here:
• Money: investing, credit, taxes, insurance, real estate and side income
• Tech: AI tools that actually save you time
• Curiosity: how everyday things are made, history's mysteries, space and science, and the psychology behind your choices

Every video is researched from cited sources, linked in its description. Visuals and narration are made with AI tools.

New explainer every day. Subscribe and get a little smarter each day.

Educational content only, not financial, legal, tax or medical advice. Talk to a licensed professional about your own situation.
```

## Studio → Settings → Channel

- **Basic info**
  - Country of residence: **India**.
  - Keywords: `explainer, personal finance, investing, credit score, real estate, money tips, AI tools, how it's made, history, mysteries, space, science, psychology, learn something new, shorts`
- **Advanced settings**
  - Audience: **No, set this channel as not made for kids**.
  - Automatic captions: on.
  - Leave the ad settings at their defaults.
- **Feature eligibility**: verify your phone number (https://www.youtube.com/verify).

## Studio → Settings → Upload defaults

- **Basic info**: visibility **Public**. Leave title, description and tags empty; the
  autopilot writes them for each video.
- **Advanced settings**
  - License: Standard YouTube License.
  - Allow embedding: on.
  - Publish to subscriptions feed: on.
  - Category: **Education**.
  - Video language: **English (United States)**.
  - Title and description language: **English (United States)**.
  - Caption certification: None.
  - Comments: **On**, with *Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review*. Sort by Top.
  - Show how many viewers like this video: on.
