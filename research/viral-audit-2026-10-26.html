Plan for the most viral Shorts a day

  1. Unblock YouTube first (you, in YouTube Studio). Open Content for the Gimli, neutron-star and mortgage Shorts and check Restrictions, Copyright and the "altered or synthetic content" answer. Check Channel status and any warning or strike. Until this is fixed, more YouTube uploads add nothing.
  2. Volume where it is rewarded. Keep YouTube at 2 a day and raise Instagram and Facebook to 3. YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy (renamed July 2025) withholds monetisation from mass-produced, templated channels, so YouTube volume can cost the Partner Program review.
  3. Three new story shapes, taken from the winners: the second-person dilemma ("You jump from a sinking boat…"), the escalating countdown ("The deadliest thing in your kitchen"), and the faceless tier ranking ("Ranking every way to pay off debt"). Each ends on a question viewers can answer in one word.
  4. Length test. Make half the week 55–70 s with an escalation every 10–15 s, and compare views per video at 72 hours.
  5. Exact numbers drawn as code. Use three.js cutaways for money and science figures, so no AI clip invents a logo or a wrong number.
  6. Switch to profit once monetised. When a platform starts paying (YouTube Partner Program, Facebook Content Monetisation), put back the ad-rate tier in niche scoring (strategy.phase). Finance and tech rankings pay up to $0.30 per 1,000 Shorts views, against $0.01–0.07 typical. Add a long-form video a week from the best Short.

The claude-youtube skill

SkillSpector (NVIDIA, static scan): risk 100, "do not install". I checked all 13 findings by hand. Twelve are false alarms: README text about settings.json, the skill's own YouTube key and OAuth files, the words "no warnings" in a policy table, an uninstaller deleting its own folder, and list-argument yt-dlp calls. The real one: the installer suggests piping uninstall.sh from GitHub into bash.

It sends nothing to unknown hosts and hides no instructions. Its data scripts need a YouTube API key, which I did not ask for. I used its Shorts and monetisation playbooks read-only and did not install it. Its playbook dates from April 2026, and two of its rules (skip 30–45 s, 3–5 hashtags) contradict this channel's own results.
