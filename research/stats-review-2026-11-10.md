# Stats review, 2026-11-10 (Metricool, 18 Oct to 9 Nov)

## Totals
- YouTube: about 9,800 views in 23 days, 16 subscribers (flat since 30 Oct). Daily views fell from 1,500-2,300
  (27-28 Oct) to 1-31 a day since 1 Nov.
- Instagram: 28 -> 27 followers. Curiosity reels 1.7-2.2K views each until 30 Oct; the last three (molasses 425,
  true fans 183, mortgage 349) fell. No reel since 2 Nov.
- Facebook: 5 followers, about 200 views a reel.

## Findings
1. Supply stopped. No new video since 2 Nov (Higgsfield at its 25-credit reserve); a 22-credit video cannot be
   made daily on this plan.
2. YouTube reposts are dead: Gimli repost 19 views in 6 days; dancing plague and emu war reposts have no row yet.
3. YouTube is hit or miss: 9 of 21 Shorts passed 450 views, the rest stayed under 50. All money topics missed.
4. Views do not convert: about 36K views across platforms made about 50 followers.
5. Distance to pay: YouTube needs 1,000 subscribers and 10M Shorts views in 90 days (20M for applicants from
   1 Feb 2027). The current pace is about 38K views per 90 days, roughly 260 times short.
6. Cheapest good video so far: "What If the Sun Disappeared?" used NASA footage for 2 of 5 blocks, cost 8.3
   credits and drew 1,337 YouTube views; the fully generated pencil video cost 23.3 for 1,404.

## Proposed pivot (same channels)
Real footage (NASA and other public-domain archives) plus three.js diagrams, with a generated voice only: a few
credits a video, no AI-artifact risk, daily posting possible. One series ("What If", space and Earth science)
until a platform pays.
