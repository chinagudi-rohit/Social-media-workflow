1. **The swipe is the bottleneck, not the watch.** Nearly half the feed swipes away at once,
   but those who stay watch 64-77% of a winner. Hooks (first frame, first five words, the
   on-screen title) are worth more than anything after second three.
2. **Curiosity reaches, money does not.** All four money Shorts (insurance, credit cards,
   $1 a day, mortgage) drew 0-8 views, whatever their retention ($1 a day kept 77.6%). The
   niche ranking now lets an untested money niche borrow the money family's results
   (`family_share`), so personal finance and legal money no longer outrank history.
3. **YouTube decides late.** Airplane windows had 2 views at 55 h and 1,313 now. A Short at
   0-1 views after 10-35 h (Gimli, mortgage, neutron star) is not yet a verdict: judge at
   72 h or later and never remake a topic for it (the name remake drew 7 against 322).
4. **Nobody comes back yet.** 99.7% new viewers and 6 follows in 4.5K views: every script
   keeps a comment prompt and a series promise ("one impossible what-if a day").
5. **Audience.** Mostly US, then the Philippines, India, UK and Canada; men 25-44 on phones.
   Captions are burned in (96% watch without subtitles), which suits them.
