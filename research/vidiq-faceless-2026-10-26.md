## Guardrail from the same sources

vidIQ's guide warns that automated channels "carry more monetization risk when videos become
repetitive or mass-produced". It also notes that photoreal AI content must be disclosed. So a
series repeats its opener and structure, never its script, topic or footage, and AI labels
stay on.
