youtu.be/8Rf0xna31xg). Pick 3-5 proven channels and take their best-performing topics. Write
a ~30 s original mini-documentary per topic, then repeat whatever earns views. The video's own

## What it means for us

- **Map surprises are the biggest open seam.** Seven of the top 18 are geography shocks
  ("Canada is further south", "France borders Brazil", "the largest city is in Mongolia").
  We have no geography niche. A map Short is exact data, which three.js draws for zero credits
  (the globe and land-dot method is in research/threejs-motion-graphics.md). That makes it
  the cheapest way to add videos a day.
- **How-it-works cutaways** ("How Electrical Outlets Work", 60M) fit our how-its-made niche
  and the 3D look.
- **"What happens if" setups** (green rope, shade balls) match our best format, the what-if.
- War and military topics, math party tricks and sexuality topics are off-brand or
  unmonetizable for us: skip them.
