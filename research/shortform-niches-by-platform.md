# Short-form niches by viewership, Western markets

Estimated ranking of niches by short-form views in Western markets (US, UK, Canada,
Australia, Western Europe), as of October 2026, for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels
and Facebook Reels. Rank 1 = most views.

## How this was ranked

No platform publishes view totals by niche, so this is an estimate, not measured data.
It weighs:

- **Top-channel charts.** Tubefilter / GospelStats weekly US Top 50: Shorts channels hold
  42-45 of 50 spots, led by challenge, family, gadget/machinery, magic, survival and
  satisfying content.
- **Hashtag and trend scale.** TikTok Creative Center trends and the TikTok Next 2026 report.
- **Audience profile per platform.**
  - TikTok skews Gen Z and young millennials.
  - Reels skews millennial lifestyle.
  - Shorts skews male 18-34, with heavy gaming, sports and kids/family viewing.
  - Facebook skews 35+, sharing heartwarming, practical and nostalgic content.
- **Industry benchmarks.** Dash Social and Socialinsider 2026 Reels reports.

Treat neighbouring ranks as roughly equal; the tiers (1-10, 11-25, 26-50) are more reliable
than exact positions.

## Views are not earnings

The top-viewed niches (comedy, gaming, dance, memes) earn the lowest RPM. The highest-RPM
niches (finance, real estate, insurance, business, AI tools) rank 34-50 on views. The best
fit for this pipeline is niches with both high views and decent RPM: life hacks, gadgets &
tech, satisfying/how-it's-made, cars, food and health. Finance niches earn per view and
convert to affiliates.
