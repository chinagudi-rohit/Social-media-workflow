## Viral video = viral idea + viral packaging + viral intro

1. **Idea: the outlier method.** A video that beats its channel's average is an outlier.
   The smaller the channel, the stronger the signal, because a small channel has no "face
   value" audience carrying its views. *Applied:* `small_channel_subs` and
   `small_channel_boost` in config/competitors.yaml, and ranking in autopilot step 1a.
   Our list is all big channels; add small faceless explainer channels whenever a listing
   finds one.
2. **Packaging: curiosity.** The title and thumbnail work together and contradict an
   assumption (Veritasium: plane door marked "NOT locked"). On Shorts, the feed shows the
   first frame and the `on_screen_hook` rather than a thumbnail. *Applied:* the
   `on_screen_hook` rule in templates/script_guide.md now says the hook contradicts and the
   title asks.
3. **Intro: goal, then problem, then promise.** Name the viewer's goal, add the fear or
   frustration that blocks it, then promise the answer. For a Short this is our spoken hook
   plus `on_screen_hook` in 5 s. No change needed.
4. **End reward** ("can give 50% more watch time"). MrBeast puts car vs cheetah in the
   title, the thumbnail and the first 5 s, then saves it for the end. "Five tips, and the
   last is the best" works the same way. *Applied:* the end reward is in the script guide's
   Build section, and it pairs with our loop ending.

## Algorithm and Studio

- Video signals: CTR, watch time, average view duration, likes, comments, shares,
  subscribers gained. Viewer signals: watch and search history, subscriptions, "not
  interested". Viewers with matching profiles get the same recommendations.
- To judge a video, use Studio's "ranking by views" (the latest upload's rank among the
  last 10 at the same age), the Reach tab (impressions, CTR, traffic sources) and the
  retention graph.

## Owner setup, desktop Studio only (27:00 in the video)

- **Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility.**
  - Intermediate features (custom thumbnails, live, videos over 15 min) need a verified
    phone number.
  - Advanced features need intermediate first, then one of three checks. He recommends
    video verification as the fastest.
- **Settings → Upload defaults.**
  - Category: Education.
  - Video, title and description language: English.
  - Automatic chapters: on.
- **Settings → Channel.** Country of residence, plus 5-6 channel keywords: the channel
  name and the niche terms.
- **Advanced settings.** "Not made for kids". This is already set.
