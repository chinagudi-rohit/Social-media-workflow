# Lessons

What this channel has learned from its own videos. The autopilot reads this before choosing
and writing scripts, and revises it after each analysis (step 1c). Each lesson names its
evidence (run ids, numbers) and its date. Drop a lesson when newer data contradicts it.
Keep it under 40 lines.

## Production

- 2026-10-18: The ElevenLabs voice reads about 2.9 words a second, so an 8-block script
  (160-180 words) runs about 55 s. Evidence: run 2026-10-18-insurance-choosing-a-car-insurance-deductible
  (159 words, 54.8 s of voice).
- 2026-10-18: Keyframe prompts that mention tags, labels or price stickers get garbled text
  ("PICS"). Describe objects without anything that carries writing. Evidence: same run,
  block 1 regenerated.
- 2026-10-18: Never pad a video past its last word. 7 s of silent ending is a swipe-away.
